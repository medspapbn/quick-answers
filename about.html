About this blog

Best Med Spa LA started for a simple reason. Friends kept texting me the same questions before their appointments. What does a med spa even do? How much does this cost? Will my face be red for a week? I answered the same things so many times that I figured I might as well write them down.

So that is what this is. Short, honest, jargon-free answers about med spa treatments, written for normal people who are curious or a little nervous. No hype. No fake reviews. No pretending a single session will change your life.

Dana Reyes

Writer, Los Angeles

I am not a doctor and I do not pretend to be one. I am a writer based in Los Angeles who got curious about the whole world of skin treatments and never stopped asking questions. Everything here comes from reading, talking to people who actually do this work, and sitting through more than a few consultations myself.

My goal is to help you walk into an appointment feeling informed instead of overwhelmed. When something needs a professional opinion, I say so. Nothing on this blog is medical advice, and the right next step for any real decision is always a licensed provider who can look at your skin in person.

If a post here saved you from a bad decision or a wasted appointment, that is the whole point.
